01 About Me

Hi, I'm Amy.

I'm a Plympton-based sports therapist and a retired competitive swimmer, so I know first-hand what training hard asks of your body. I graduated in 2025 with a BSc (Hons) in Sports Therapy, and I've built up over four years of hands-on experience, from private clients and clinical placements at Plymouth Marjon University to pitchside work with Newton Abbot Ladies Rugby and Plympton Victoria Rugby.

Whether you're an athlete looking for recovery support, you have a long-term injury you'd like to sort out, or you simply need to ease those everyday muscle aches, I'm here to help.

  • BSc (Hons) Sports Therapy
  • Also qualified through the Federation of Holistic Therapists with a Level 4 Diploma in Soft Tissue Therapy
  • A proud member of the Sports Therapy Organisation, ensuring the highest standards of professionalism and care in my practice
  • Fully insured through Balens, a specialist insurance broker

03 What's on offer

Treatments & prices

Every session is tailored to you, your sport and your goals. No packages to wade through: just book what you need.

  1. Sports massage 45 min – 1 hr

    Bespoke treatment catered to your needs, whether that's enhancing recovery, performance and mobility, or simply switching off and relaxing.

    Price: £28

  2. Injury assessment Initial session

    A thorough one-to-one session to find the root cause of your injury, assess mobility and function, and build a tailored recovery plan. Ideal for first-time clients or new injuries.

    Price: £32

  3. Injury rehab Follow-up

    Follow-up sessions that support your recovery, improve movement and track your progress, each one building on your personalised plan for safe, effective rehabilitation.

    Price: £26

  4. Kinesiology taping Add-on

    Expert application using high-quality tape, added to any session. Provides lasting injury and posture support and helps athletic performance.
